Decide · War Game · standalone

War Game — pressure-test a decision

Its own surface, three modes. Start from a scenario (in an engagement) or standalone: pick the teams + the trigger and play. All three read the same teams / moves / payoff substrate.

Guided · turn-based
You play a move per team; the adversary reacts. Round-by-round, human-in-the-loop. Best for facilitated workshops.
live
Game theory
Build the payoff matrix, solve for the Nash cell, show the equilibrium reasoning. Best for "what's the rational move?"
partial · PB-H12
Autonomous simulation
Agents play it out N rounds unattended → a move/counter-move transcript + an outcome distribution. Best for "run it 100×."
planned · PB-H13
Seeded from scenario “Competitive pressure: Dulaglutide” · teams: Novo · Lilly · Payers · each move carries a per-team impact vector (PB-H11, live). Promote the winning round → a Decision.
New standalone war game →

Substrate exists across war_game_engine.py, war_game_adversary.py (reactive), game_theory.py (Stackelberg/Bayesian), simulation/payoff.py. The surface unifies them under one mode picker; PB-H12 (3×3 Nash) and PB-H13 (autonomous play) complete modes 2–3.
