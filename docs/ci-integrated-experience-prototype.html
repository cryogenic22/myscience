Market Zero · Competitive Intelligence · Information-architecture prototype

Bringing the experience together

Today the cockpit has nine flat tabs organised by artifact (three feeds, a watchlist, engagements, war rooms, decisions, insights, reviewer). This prototype reorganises them around the sense → decide → act → learn flywheel, with two primary modes and one missing connector: the promote bridge that turns a signal into work. Click the nav and the view toggles to explore.

Insights & calibration

Where outcomes feed back. Decision outcomes re-weight source trust; observed signals re-calibrate scenario priors — and that changes what Sense surfaces first.
