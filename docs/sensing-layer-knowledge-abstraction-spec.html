MARKET ZERO · SENSING LAYER DESIGN SPEC · 3 JUN 2026

The signal is a lens, not a store

A design for making the sensing layer a curated abstraction over the unified knowledge store — so that as we feed it richer data (a conference deck, an annual report, a company’s own history), the facts, the knowledge graph, the signals, and the synthesis all get richer together, through one spine. Companion to data-hub-agentic-layer-analysis.html and the Helix v8 sensing model. Grounded in the shipped DR-loop fact substrate (DR-1/3/4/6/7/9), the facts ledger, the signals table, and dossier_kb.

The thesis in one line.

A fact is the atom of the knowledge store. A signal is not a separate kind of thing — it is a fact (or cluster of facts) the sense layer has scored, timed, and surfaced because it matters now. Today they live in two parallel tables that don’t reference each other. The design unifies them: signals reference the facts they feed (Helix v8’s feeds_fact_ids), the knowledge store stays the single source of truth, and every new document deepens both at once.

1 · Where we are — two parallel stores

The substrate work this session filled the facts ledger from a 2-predicate monoculture (3,303 rows) to a 9-predicate, evidence-bearing knowledge base (9,679 facts / 5,709 evidence records) via the DR-loop fact emitters. But the signal layer never connected to it — it derives from market_events and scores them, on a track of its own.

TODAY — parallel, disconnected market_events ──▶ signals (037) facts ledger (065) ◀── DR-1/3/4/6/7/9 emitters │ kbq_tags, trust, │ predicate, subject, object_value, │ impact, status │ fact_class, confidence, valid_from, │ │ │ source_doc_id ─▶ evidence_records ▼ ▼ ▼ Digest Stream / Signals DB Dossier (8 domains) (reads market_events) (reads signals) (reads facts + signals + metrics) ▲ no link between a signal and the facts it implies ▲

Consequences we already felt this session: the digest flooded with 2,688×-duplicated recall events (fixed by read-time dedup); the Signals DB couldn’t show why a signal matters (no link to the underlying facts); and the rich new facts (mechanism, literature, trial readouts) don’t surface as signals at all.

2 · The target — one substrate, many lenses

One knowledge store. The signal layer, the dossier, the KBQ view, and the scenario priors are all lenses over it.

A lens adds curation metadata (scoring, timing, grouping, KBQ tag) but never owns the truth. Delete a lens and the knowledge is intact; add a lens and it reads the same facts a new way.

The signal lens becomes a thin, queryable, curated projection: “the facts that changed recently, scored by impact × trust, grouped, and tagged to a business question.” That is precisely the “curated for CI / KBQ” abstraction you described.

3 · The unified data model

3.1 · The fact is the atom (exists today)

fact { predicate // clinical_trial · mechanism_of_action · key_publication · // trial_result · label_indication · wac_usd · market_event … subject_entity // (drug | company | trial | …, id) ← entity-resolved object_value // jsonb: the claim + structured fields fact_class // reference | corporate | signal | inferred | internal confidence // 0..1 valid_from / to // temporal source_doc_id ───▶ evidence_record { source_id, source_url, extracted_text, hash } }

3.2 · The fact_class colour system (adopt Helix v8 verbatim)

Legibility comes from meaning. Every fact and every signal is coloured by where it came from — the single most important change to the “all-black” Signals DB:

R reference  regulatory baseline, epidemiology, MeSH mechanism, peer-reviewed lit    C corporate  10-K/10-Q/8-K, label, press release, conference deck
S signal  monitored / time-bound (FAERS trend, news event)    I inferred  analyst / modelled / LLM-derived    X internal  ZS / client tenant-scoped (KOL panel, field notes)

3.3 · The one new join: signal ↔ fact

The only schema addition needed to unify the layers — the v8 feeds_fact_ids edge:

-- new: the link that makes a signal a lens over facts signal_facts ( signal_id uuid → signals.id, fact_id uuid → facts.id, role text // 'produces' | 'corroborates' | 'contradicts' | 'relates' ) -- a signal now answers "which facts did I change?" ▸ provenance forward -- a fact now answers "which signals surfaced me?" ▸ provenance backward

Signals can then be emitted from fact deltas, not only from market_events: when a DR emitter asserts a high-impact fact (a phase-3 readout, a boxed warning, a price change), a signal is minted that produces it. The two stores become one graph.

5 · Progressive enrichment — how each document deepens everything

This is the heart of the design. Follow one asset (CagriSema, in the Novo obesity wargame) as data is added. Each addition flows through the same spine (resolve → fact → evidence → graph edge → signal → synthesis). Nothing is bolted on; the substrate compounds.

T0 · Public substrate (where we are after the DR-loops)

Public connectors + DR emitters give the baseline. CagriSema / semaglutide already carry:

R mechanism_of_action (DR-6)   R key_publication ×N (DR-7)   C clinical_trial ×N (DR-1)   S adverse_event (DR-3)   C label_indication (DR-4)

Dossier coverage: clinical_profile strong; disease & patient seeded; pricing, commercial, HCP, internal — thin or empty.

T1 · + Upload a conference-readout deck (PPTX) — DR-9

ECO 2026 — REDEFINE 4 readout.pptx (drag onto Sources) → document_extractor: slides + tables + speaker notes [DR-9 P1 ✓] → extract_document_facts (LLM): C trial_result [DR-9 P2 ✓] "Phase 3, primary endpoint not met, in obesity (REDEFINE 4) — 23%" → resolve_asset_to_subject("CagriSema") ─▶ drug:cagrisema (richest row) → assert_fact + evidence_record (slide text, hash) [ledger +1, evidence +1] → NEW GRAPH EDGE: cagrisema ──trial_result──▶ REDEFINE 4 → NEW SIGNAL minted, role=produces, impact=high (primary missed) → SYNTHESIS: clinical_profile fact count ↑; Scenario A/D priors shift

A single deck became: 1 fact + 1 evidence record + 1 graph edge + 1 high-impact signal + a scenario-prior nudge — all entity-resolved and provenanced, with zero new wiring beyond the upload.

T2 · + Upload the Novo annual report (10-K / 6-K PDF) — DR-9 + SEC

Novo Nordisk FY2025 annual report.pdf → document_extractor: per-page text + tables (financials) [DR-9 P1 ✓] → financial extraction (existing SEC 8-K schemas): revenue, guidance → facts: C revenue · C sales_guidance · subject company:novo-nordisk → entity_links: novo ──owns──▶ semaglutide ──competes──▶ tirzepatide → FILLS commercial_operational domain (was empty) → NEW SIGNALS: "Novo FY guidance raised", "Wegovy revenue +56%" → SYNTHESIS: KBQ-5 (Sales & Sentiment) flips insufficient → fresh

The same uploader, a different document shape, fills a different domain. The knowledge graph now spans clinical and commercial — the dossier can reason about efficacy-vs-revenue trade-offs it couldn’t before.

T3 · + Add the company’s own history & field intelligence (internal)

Past launch retrospective.pptx · KOL advisory notes · field call log → same pipeline, X internal fact_class, tenant-scoped (engagement_sources) → facts: X kol_stance · X payer_signal · I launch_lesson "KOLs require H2H CV outcome before switching" (N=80, 61%) → entity_links: cagrisema ──hcp_barrier──▶ "head-to-head requirement" → FILLS hcp_and_patient domain; corroborates Scenario D → SYNTHESIS: cross-class triangulation — a C corporate efficacy gap + an X internal KOL barrier = a sharper, defensible insight

This is the differentiator: public + corporate data is the same for every competitor; the client’s own history and field intelligence is unique. Once it’s in the same graph with the same provenance, synthesis can weigh it against public evidence.

Why this design enables synthesis.

Because every addition lands as a typed, classed, time-stamped, entity-resolved, evidence-backed fact in one graph, synthesis is just reading the graph through a lens: the dossier reads it by domain, the KBQ view by business question, the scenario engine by prior-delta, the signal lens by recency × impact. Add a document → the facts appear → every lens that touches those entities gets richer automatically. No lens needs to know where the data came from.

6 · How synthesis reads the enriched substrate

Dossier lens

assemble_dossier_for_asset routes each fact to one of 8 ZS domains by predicate. More fact classes → more domains cross the readiness threshold → fewer gaps. The deck filled clinical; the 10-K filled commercial; the internal notes filled HCP.

KBQ lens

kbq_views maps facts+signals to 8 business questions. The “curated for CI” entry point: ask “What’s the competitive picture?” and get the classed, sourced facts that answer it — the query surface you asked for.

Scenario lens

PB-H14 writes scenarios.current_prob from fact deltas. A new high-impact corporate readout fact shifts a prior; the war-game re-weights. Signals become the change-detector for priors.

Signal lens

Reads the same facts by recency × impact × trust, grouped + KBQ-tagged. Every signal carries feeds_fact_ids so the user can always drill from “what changed” to “the evidence behind it.”

The one idea to take away.

Don’t build a better signals table. Make the signal a lens over the facts we’re already accumulating, link every signal to the facts it feeds, colour everything by where it came from, and let every uploaded document deepen the one graph. Then “making the sensing layer richer” stops being a feature you build and becomes a property that emerges every time data arrives.
