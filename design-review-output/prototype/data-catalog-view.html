Bring your own data · request a connector · build a custom one.

Every catalog needs an "add to it" door. Three doors here: drag-and-drop document upload (auto-annotated through the five-stage pipeline), the connector marketplace (15 active + 28 in roadmap, vote for what to prioritise next), and the custom-connector SDK.

↑
Upload documents
Drop a PDF, DOCX, transcript, or research note. The five-stage pipeline runs automatically — extraction, NER, resolution cascade, embedding, cross-link. Queryable in ~10 seconds. Tenant-isolated.
⊕
Request a connector
Browse the 28-source roadmap. Vote for what your TA most needs. Every team's request shifts the priority queue. Steward reviews monthly.
⚙
Build a custom connector
Inherit BaseConnector. Set source_key, schedule, license profile. Framework handles fetch, normalize, resolve, embed, store, link. Approval review by data steward before production.

A data catalog is not a list of tables. It is the surface where the substrate becomes legible, tunable, and buildable-on.
Five sections, three personas. The exec leaves with a number for the substrate's size and health. The analyst leaves having found the entity they need with full source attribution. The data steward leaves having cleared the queue, audited the licences, and approved the BYOD uploads. The same surface served all three — because the catalog is not a steward-only tool. It's the load-bearing surface of every conversation about what the platform knows.
